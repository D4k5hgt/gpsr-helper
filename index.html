What this tool does

This is not legal advice. It is an organiser and checklist based on the EU General Product Safety Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2023/988). You are responsible for checking that your products and listings comply. If you are unsure, ask a qualified professional.

Since 13 December 2024, consumer products sold to people in the EU must meet GPSR rules, wherever the seller is based. For each product this tool helps you:

  • See whether GPSR probably applies, and which other EU rules you should look into
  • Write the safety information block that online listings must show
  • Write the identification text for your product label or packaging
  • Fill in a basic written risk assessment you can print and keep
  • Work out whether selling to the EU is worth the compliance cost

Your data stays on this device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere: there are no accounts and no cookies, and visits are only counted anonymously. See the privacy policy.
