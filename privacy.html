Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who runs this tool

GPSR Helper is run by Daksh, India. Contact: dakshgta81929@gmail.com.

The short version

We do not collect, receive or store any of the information you type into GPSR Helper. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no payments, no advertising, no cookies, and no AI services. We count visits anonymously so we know whether the tool is useful (see "Visitor statistics" below).

Where your information is kept

The details you enter (your business details, your EU Responsible Person's details, your products, risk assessments and calculator figures) are saved only in your own browser on your own device, using the browser's "local storage". They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Because the information stays on your device:

  • We cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you.
  • You can delete it at any time with My data → Delete all my data, or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • If you download a backup file, that file is stored wherever you save it, under your control.

Visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused statistics service, to count how many people use the tool. When you visit, GoatCounter receives:

  • the page you visited and the website that linked you here (if any)
  • your browser, operating system, screen size and approximate country (worked out from your IP address)
  • the names of a few actions, such as "product added", "listing text copied" or "printed"

GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store your IP address. It does not build a profile of you or track you across other websites. It never receives anything you type into the tool: no business details, product names, warnings or figures. We use these statistics only to see how many people use the tool and which features help. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

To stop being counted, you can block gc.zgo.at with any content blocker. The tool works the same without it.

Third parties

Apart from GoatCounter (above) and our web host (below), the tool does not load content from or share data with any third party. Links to outside websites (such as EUR-Lex) only open if you click them, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When your browser requests the page, GitHub may automatically record technical information (such as your IP address) for security and to deliver the site. We do not have access to this information and do not use it to identify you. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Children

GPSR Helper is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data you enter into the tool, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. The visitor statistics are anonymous counts that can't be linked back to you. For questions about the statistics, the hosting information above, or anything else in this policy, contact dakshgta81929@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change how the tool handles information (for example, if we ever add accounts or payments), we will update this policy before the change takes effect and change the "last updated" date.
